## 📚 A Quick Guide to the Science (for the Curious)

 
If you are the type whol likes to understand the "why" behind things, here is a gentle introduction:

The internet works by breaking your data into small pieces called packets. These packets travel through cables and routers to reach their destination. Normally, they go directly via the fastest route. But sometimes, you want to change how they travel. Maybe you want to make them take a more secure path, or you want to test how different routes perform. OpenFlux lets you create a tunnel—a special pathway for your packets. Inside that tunnel, you can choose different "transports" ore packaging methods. Some methods wrap your data in extra security. Others compress it to make it faster. You can switch between them and watch the results in real time. It is a wonderful way to learn how networks really behave without needing a cybersecurity degree.
